About Mike

Born and raised in the heart of Northwest Georgia, Mike Bolen brings a sound that’s as genuine as the red clay roads he grew up on. With a voice steeped in tradition and songs that speak to real life, love, and hard work, Mike’s music carries the storytelling spirit of classic country while keeping it true to who he is.

Drawing inspiration from legends like George Strait, Alan Jackson, and Randy Travis, Mike blends timeless country roots with a touch of his own Southern grit. Whether he’s playing a small-town honky-tonk or lighting up a festival stage, he connects with every crowd through honesty, heart, and a good dose of Georgia charm.
